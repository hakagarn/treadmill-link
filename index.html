Manual control

Start the belt from the console at a walking pace, or use Start belt below, and keep your hands on the rails. The safety key still stops the belt, so wear the clip.

Tap Start belt again within 5 seconds to start. Many treadmills count down for a few seconds first, so stand on the side rails until the belt moves.
